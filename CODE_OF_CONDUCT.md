# Code of Conduct

## Expected behavior

Project participants should communicate respectfully, focus reviews on the
work, provide reproducible evidence for technical claims, welcome good-faith
questions, and avoid harassment, discrimination or disclosure of private data.

## Unacceptable behavior

Threats, targeted abuse, discriminatory language, sexual harassment, doxxing,
credential disclosure, deliberate benchmark contamination and sustained
bad-faith disruption are not accepted.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the active maintainer listed in
`MAINTAINERS.md`. Do not include secrets or unrelated personal data. The
maintainer may warn, moderate or exclude participants when necessary to protect
the community and project. Security vulnerabilities must use `SECURITY.md`
instead of this process.
